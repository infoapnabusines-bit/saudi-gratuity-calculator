# Which Allowances Are Included in Saudi Gratuity? (Article 2 Breakdown)

One of the most frequent labor disputes filed via the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (MHRSD) involves whether allowances should be included in final settlement calculations.

## The Legal Meaning of "Actual Wage" (الأجر الفعلي)

Article 2 of the Saudi Labor Law defines the "actual wage" as the basic salary plus all allowances, stipends, and increments granted to a worker in consideration of their labor.

### 1. Mandatory Allowances to Include
* **Basic Salary:** The registered core wage.
* **Housing Allowance (بدل سكن):** Paid monthly, quarterly, or annually. If housing is provided in-kind, its designated cash equivalent must be reflected.
* **Transportation Allowance (بدل مواصلات):** Regular recurring transport allowance.
* **Fixed Periodic Commission / Regular Bonuses:** If guaranteed unconditionally every month.

### 2. Excluded Non-Wage Payments
* Reimbursements for operational business expenses (travel per-diems, fuel receipts).
* Discretionary annual performance bonuses not guaranteed in the employment contract.
* Overtime wages (which are remunerated separately under Article 107).
